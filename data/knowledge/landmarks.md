# HCMC Architectural Landmarks Guide

## Nhà Thờ Đức Bà (Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon)
Constructed between 1863 and 1880 by French colonists, Notre-Dame Cathedral stands prominently in Paris Square in District 1. Built entirely with materials imported from France—including vibrant red Marseille bricks that retain their color to this day and twin 58-meter neo-Romanesque bell towers. In front of the cathedral stands the Virgin Mary marble statue installed in 1959. Note that extensive restoration work has been underway, so interior access may be restricted, but the exterior remains a favorite photography spot.

## Bưu Điện Trung Tâm Sài Gòn (Saigon Central Post Office)
Located directly across from Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Saigon Central Post Office was completed in 1891. Designed in French colonial style with intricate wrought-iron arches reminiscent of Gustave Eiffel's architecture, the grand interior features patterned tile floors, vintage wooden phone booths, large hand-painted maps of South Vietnam and Cambodia from 1892, and a prominent portrait of President Ho Chi Minh. It remains a functioning post office where visitors can send handwritten postcards internationally.

## Dinh Độc Lập (Independence Palace / Reunification Convention Hall)
Set in a 12-hectare lush park on Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street, the Independence Palace was designed by architect Ngo Viet Thu and served as the home and workplace of the President of South Vietnam until April 30, 1975, when North Vietnamese tank #390 crashed through its gates. The building preserves remarkable mid-century modernist architecture, including banquet halls, the President's office, private cinema, rooftop helipad, and underground war telecommunications bunker with vintage radio equipment and secret escape tunnels.

## Tòa Nhà Landmark 81
Standing at 461.3 meters along the Saigon River in Binh Thanh District, Landmark 81 is the tallest skyscraper in Vietnam and one of the tallest in Southeast Asia. Its architectural design is inspired by the traditional Vietnamese bamboo bundle, symbolizing strength, resilience, and unity. The complex includes the Vincom Center luxury shopping mall, ice skating rink, 5-star Autograph Collection hotel, and the Landmark 81 SkyView observation deck on floors 79 to 81 offering 360-degree panoramic views of Ho Chi Minh City.
